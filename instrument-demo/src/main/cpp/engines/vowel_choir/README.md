# Vowel Choir

Vowel Choir adapts the six-singer oscillator from [GR!MM's VOICE](https://github.com/GRiMMBIENT/grimm/blob/b519acbee7dbdb3e0fb82b15b86a913898c6f8f5/firmware/src/formant.c) at commit `b519acbee7dbdb3e0fb82b15b86a913898c6f8f5`, under GPL-3.0-only. The port moves the oscillator and its drift, vibrato, breath, vowel morphing, interpolation and band limiting into Skiff's per-track voice state. It adds individual singer selections and original Instrument presets.

**Choir recordings** by Julia Wilkins, Prem Seetharaman, Alison Wahl and Bryan Pardo, Northwestern University, 2018. [VocalSet: A Singing Voice Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1442513) is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**Wavetable modifications** by GR!MM. It measured the first 64 harmonics from the steady portions of two notes for each of the five vowels sung by m6, m3, m4, f7, f9 and f4. It rephased the harmonics per singer, normalized their levels, emphasized the resonances of E, I and U, and generated four band-limited 8-bit single-cycle tables per recorded note and vowel. [GR!MM describes the processing and its attribution](https://github.com/GRiMMBIENT/grimm/blob/b519acbee7dbdb3e0fb82b15b86a913898c6f8f5/LICENSING.md#the-choir-wavetables-vocalset). Instrument includes the donor's `grimm_choir.h` unchanged (28,800 bytes of wavetable samples), with no original recordings. The dataset, its authors and singers do not endorse this port.
