# CHUA

This Instrument engine adapts [HAWK CHUA](https://github.com/Plieuw/hawk-chua/tree/e750df6a2e13490a4f01b84d3f9c553f4612163f) by Plieuw and contributors, pinned at `e750df6a2e13490a4f01b84d3f9c553f4612163f`. The tables are the donor's `firmware/src/chua_tables.h` without changes. The circuit and two noise algorithms adapt `firmware/src/synth.c` and `firmware/src/noise.c`. `thirdparty/hawk_waveguide.inc` adapts the VOICE, FLUTE, BRASS and REED sections from `firmware/src/waveguide.c`. The individual donor sources carry GPL-3.0-only with the donor's additional permission for linking against M-VAVE's binary SDK. Instrument's combined Skiff code is GPL-3.0-only. See `LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt` and `LICENSE-linking-exception.md`. Instrument uses Skiff's envelopes, pitch, allocation and effects, with no binary SDK, donor firmware UI or donor preset bank.

**CHUA** runs the donor's Q20 midpoint solver and interpolates the per-regime alpha, period and RMS tables. CHAOS moves from a limit cycle through period division to chaotic scrolls and the outer cycle. SHAPE changes the circuit's beta. OUTPUT blends the circuit's X, Y and Z. Its four-substep limit also limits the oscillator at very high notes.

**RESONOISE** applies square-wave FM, wavefolding and a noise-modulated band-pass. **ARRAY ROCKS** scrambles a 64-point wavetable while sine-wave FM modulates its playback. Both use the instrument's RATE and model-specific controls. They are distinct from Skiff's analog noise and shift-register sources.

**TRACT**, **FLUTE**, **REED** and **BRASS** feed the Chua oscillator into the donor's acoustic models. TRACT uses a Kelly–Lochbaum vocal tract with five vowel shapes. FLUTE models an air jet and open bore. REED supports both a cylindrical bore and conical double-delay bore. BRASS uses buzzing lips, a resonant bore and an adjustable bell mute. Model parameters change their labels with the mode. Skiff adds nine original presets and supports four simultaneous voices. The port includes 1,666 bytes of numeric circuit tables and no recorded sounds.
